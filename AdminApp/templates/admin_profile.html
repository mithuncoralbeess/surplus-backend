Password Security Rules

To protect sensitive surplus management operations, admin passwords must comply with modern complexity standards:

  • Minimum 8 characters
  • At least 1 uppercase letter (A-Z)
  • At least 1 lowercase letter (a-z)
  • At least 1 number digit (0-9)
  • At least 1 special character (!@#$...)
  • Changing password terminates other active sessions
